# Four-Day Programme Agenda

## Day 1 — Databricks Foundations, Enterprise Data & Machine Learning

**First half:** workspace, notebooks, compute, catalogs/schemas/tables/files, notebook import, database loading, SQL/Python exploration, supporting documents and troubleshooting.

**Second half:** time-series/sensor data, preparation, visualisation, ML foundations, features/target, train/test, regression, MAE, forecasting and interpretation.

**Hands-on outcome:** working A-001 notebook from enterprise data to short-term vibration forecast.

## Day 2 — Generative AI & Retrieval-Augmented Generation

**First half:** traditional ML vs GenAI, LLMs, prompts, tokens/context, foundation models, model parameters, structured output, limitations and hallucinations.

**Second half:** document ingestion, chunking, embeddings, semantic retrieval / Databricks AI Search concepts, metadata, versions, approval status, source attribution and citations.

**Hands-on outcome:** enterprise A-001 RAG assistant grounded in the supplied manuals, procedures and reports.

## Day 3 — Agentic AI & Multi-Agent Systems

**First half:** LLM/RAG/agents, tool calling, structured-data tools, RAG as knowledge capability, analysis tools, routing, multi-step workflows, state/context, error handling and human-in-the-loop.

**Second half:** supervisor/specialist-agent patterns, delegation, shared state, specialist roles, tracing/evaluation, permissions/governance and responsible Agentic AI.

**Outcome:** a governed agent capable of combining structured enterprise data and document-based knowledge, plus an understanding of when multi-agent architecture is appropriate.

## Day 4 — Integrated Enterprise AI Application, Capstone & Evaluation

**First half:** combine structured data, analytics, ML outputs, enterprise documents/RAG, agents/tools, governance and human review.

**Second half:** guided capstone, evaluation, validation, evidence gaps, governance/oversight and participant demonstrations.

**Outcome:** end-to-end understanding of how enterprise data, ML, GenAI, RAG and Agentic AI combine into a governed enterprise application.
