# Synthetic Training Notice

Everything in this repository is designed for education and demonstration.

- Asset A-001 is fictional.
- The Northstar Process Facility references are fictional.
- Policies, procedures, work orders, reports and observations are synthetic.
- Sensor readings and forecasts are synthetic.
- The material is not an engineering instruction.
- The material must not be used to operate, diagnose, control or authorise work on real equipment.
- AI outputs in this repository are decision-support examples only.

The programme deliberately teaches human review, authority boundaries, evidence provenance and auditability.
