# Capstone Evaluation Rubric

Score each dimension from **1 to 5**. Maximum score: **25**.

| Dimension | What to evaluate |
|---|---|
| Problem understanding | Did the participant correctly frame the business question and avoid jumping straight to a technical answer? |
| Data use | Did they use structured data appropriately and distinguish observations from interpretation? |
| AI / RAG use | Did they retrieve appropriate knowledge and respect document approval/version status? |
| Evidence & explainability | Can the participant show where the important claims came from and identify limitations? |
| Governance & human oversight | Did they preserve uncertainty and keep final operational authority with a human reviewer? |

## Interpretation

- **21–25:** strong end-to-end understanding
- **16–20:** good working understanding with minor gaps
- **11–15:** partial understanding; important concepts need reinforcement
- **5–10:** requires guided remediation

The score is for training feedback only; it is not an operational competency certification.
