# A-001 Capstone Brief

## Scenario

An engineer has observed unusual behaviour around synthetic Asset **A-001**. Your task is to prepare an evidence-backed condition/reliability review using the available enterprise data and knowledge.

## Business question

**What does the available evidence say about A-001, what should a qualified reviewer pay attention to, and what is the appropriate human next step?**

## Your workflow

1. Understand the business question.
2. Inspect the relevant structured data.
3. Review the recent sensor trend / ML output.
4. Retrieve relevant enterprise documents.
5. Confirm which procedure/policy is currently authoritative.
6. Combine evidence without blurring source types.
7. Identify conflicts, limitations or missing evidence.
8. Prepare a bounded review briefing.
9. Stop at the human-review boundary.

## Required output

### 1. Situation
What triggered the review?

### 2. Structured evidence
What do the database and sensor/history records show?

### 3. Predictive / analytical evidence
What does the ML/forecasting output suggest, and what does it **not** prove?

### 4. Document evidence
Which approved documents are relevant? Include IDs/versions/status.

### 5. Assessment
What can reasonably be concluded from the combined evidence?

### 6. Evidence gaps / uncertainty
What is still unknown, conflicting or unverified?

### 7. Recommended human next step
What should a qualified reviewer inspect, confirm or decide next?

### 8. Sources used
List the tables/views and document IDs used.

## Boundary

This is a synthetic training exercise. The final result is a **decision-support briefing**, not an equipment diagnosis or maintenance authorisation.
